## Not legal advice

These pages describe Novi Corpus and how it works. They are not legal advice. Novi Corpus is not a law firm: for advice about your company or your agent, ask your own lawyer.

## What Novi Corpus is not

- Novi Corpus is the record. The owner of an agent answers for it; Novi Corpus records which company stands behind the agent and signs answers about that record.
- Novi Corpus never holds your agent's money, keys or identity, and it cannot change or dissolve a legal body on its own.
- A signed answer says that a registered legal body stands behind an agent and where it stands, and nothing more. It says nothing about the company's finances, what the agent does, or how well.

## Test answers

Arc testnet and the sandbox of the legal-body flow are for testing. In the sandbox, the check of a declared company is automatic and checks nothing, so a sandbox answer says nothing about any company. Test funds have no value.
