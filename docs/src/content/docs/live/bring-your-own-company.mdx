This door is for an owner who already has a company and an agent. The company stays yours: Novi Corpus records that it stands behind the agent and that you answer for it. Our team checks the company against the state's record.

## The steps

<Steps>

1. Sign in and show you are one real person with World ID; an identity check then says who. Your name is not published.
2. Declare your company: its name and its Wyoming filing number. Our team checks it against the state's record.
3. Approve the agreement and the declaration with your signature. <a href="/live/legal-texts/">The agreement and the declaration</a>
4. Link your agent: its public identity names the legal body, and the legal body names the agent.

</Steps>
