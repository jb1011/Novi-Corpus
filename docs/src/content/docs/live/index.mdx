Minimal Novi gives your agent a registered legal body. The company is a Wyoming DAO LLC we form for you, or a Wyoming LLC you already own. One person, the guardian, answers for it. The agent's public identity and the legal body name each other, and anyone can check the link in a signed answer.

We never hold your agent's money, keys or identity. We cannot change or dissolve a legal body on our own.
