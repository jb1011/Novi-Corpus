This door is for an owner who has an agent and no company yet. A company formed through us has its DAO statement added by our filing provider. You become its guardian, and the agent represents it.

## The steps

<Steps>

1. Sign in and show you are one real person with World ID; an identity check then says who.
2. Choose the company's name and pay the formation fee.
3. Our filing provider files the company with Wyoming, with its DAO statement. This takes a few working days; the waiting screen shows each step as the state confirms it.
4. Once the filing is confirmed, approve the agreement and link your agent: its public identity and the legal body name each other.

</Steps>

## What you get

<CardGrid cols={3}>
	<Card icon="building" title="The company">
		A Wyoming DAO LLC formed for your agent, with you as its guardian.
	</Card>
	<Card icon="idcard" title="A public record">

	</Card>
	<Card icon="clock" title="Time to think">

	</Card>
</CardGrid>
