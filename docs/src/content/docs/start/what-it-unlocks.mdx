Today most AI agents are helpers. An agent acts through its owner's accounts and in its owner's name, and nobody can hold it to anything: whoever deals with it deals with a key.

Novi Corpus makes agents actors. An agent with a Novi Corpus legal body represents a company, a Wyoming DAO LLC, which can sign, contract, invoice, hire and be hired, pay and be paid, and be held liable, with one person who answers for it, checked as one real person.

## Why a legal body, not a card

With an API key, the agent's acts are its owner's. A card adds a scope to pay within; the acts stay its owner's. With a legal body, they become the company's acts, in its own name, once the permission is published.
